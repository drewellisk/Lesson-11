Big Walk Review

By Drew Kopp

August 9th, 2026

Big Walk is a cooperative adventure and puzzle game for 2-12 players in which your group solves puzzles to build structures across the map. While playing, you'll need to learn how to communicate with your group using gestures and signals. Much like the popular 2023 game Lethal Company, the built-in proximity chat raises the stakes and forces your group to stick together.

While playing, you'll encounter a number of cleverly designed puzzles to gain important new items that progress your journey. These puzzles are challenging yet fun. Don't worry about failing, often times the most amusing parts of the game are when things go wrong!

This adventure game is utterly unique and absurdly charming. It brings your group together in a very special way. In those moments where you get seperated or lost, you feel a true sense of loneliness and dread. The moment when you finally reunite makes you you appreciate the bonds and camaraderie among your group.

Big Walk is a GameSpace_ 9/10 game. An incredibly impactful romp through a quirky world that truly strengthens your bonds with your friends. It provides a fair challenge with a solid progression system to keep you hooked. The proximity chat brings everyone together, it's only together that you and your friends will survive this Big Walk.
