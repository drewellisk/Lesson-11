Red Dead Redemption 2: All Bandit Challenges

By Drew Kopp

August 9th, 2026

Red Dead Redemption 2 offers many challenges that are necessary to 100% the game. The Bandit challenges revolve around committing crimes across the map.

Challenge

Solution
Bandit Challenge 1 Hold Up 5 townsfolk. Walk up to an NPC and point your gun. Press the button to 'rob'. Wear your mask to avoid recognition.
Bandit Challenge 2 Rob 2 coachs or return 2 coaches to the fence. The wagon fence is located at Emerald Ranch. Ride around the area until a coach wagon spawns. Steal the wagon without witnesses and sell it to the fence. Repeat.
Bandit Challenge 3 Rob the cash register in 4 shops in one day. Begin in Strawberry, rob the general store, then ride to Valentine. Rob the general store in Valentine, then the gunsmith, ride directly to Rhodes and rob the gunsmith.
Bandit Challenge 4 Return 3 coaches to the fence in one day. This challenge is almost identical to the second challenge. The catch is, you ahve to finish in one day. Head to Emerald Ranch late at night. Watch the time and begin the challenge at midnight.
Bandit Challenge 5 Amass a 250 dollar bounty in any state. This is a simple one. Just commit crimes without leaving state lines. Try not to get caught to save time.
Bandit Challenge 6 Steal and sell 5 horse to the fence. This is NOT the wagon fence. It is the horse fence west of Rhodes. Ride around the nearby path and steal horses from passerbys. Sell them directly to the fence.
Bandit Challenge 7 Rob 50 dollars from townsfolk. This can be done anywhere and does not have a time limit. Find a train at a station and hide in the back. When it starts moving, walk along the train and rob the passengers.
Bandit Challenge 8 Return 7 wagons to the fence. Repeat the process from challenges 2 and 4 a total of 7 times.
Bandit Challenge 9 Hogtie 3 people and leave them on the tracks. Just wait at a crossroads between road and track. When people pass by, hogtie them and throw them on the tracks. The train does not have to hit them.
Bandit Challenge 10 Rob 5 trains without being caught. Seems difficult, but all you need to do is rob one person. Buy a train ticket, then rob one person on the train and immediately jump off. Repeat 5 times. Avoid being caught.
